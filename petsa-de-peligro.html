About Petsa De Peligro

Petsa De Peligro is a simple budget and bill tracker built for non-techy users. A guided setup asks about income, bills, and monthly budget, then the app keeps track of what is due and what is left. No passwords to remember, just a personal link that works on any phone. It already has real customers using it.

Who it’s for

  • Individuals and families
  • Freelancers with irregular income
  • Anyone tired of “petsa de peligro”
  • Budget-conscious households

Key features

  • Guided first-time setup
  • Income and expense logging
  • Bill due-date tracking
  • Monthly budget overview
  • No passwords, personal link access
  • Mobile-friendly, works on any phone
