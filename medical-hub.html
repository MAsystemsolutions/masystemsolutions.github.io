About Medical Hub

Medical Hub gives small clinics an organized, searchable patient database without expensive medical software. Staff can manage patient profiles, record visits and notes, and see daily clinic activity at a glance. Data lives in the clinic's own Google account.

Who it’s for

  • Small and private clinics
  • Dental and therapy practices
  • Wellness centers
  • Medical administrators

Key features

  • Patient profiles and contact records
  • Visit history and clinical notes
  • Appointment tracking
  • Clinic operations dashboard
  • Fast search across patients
  • Runs on the clinic’s own Google account
