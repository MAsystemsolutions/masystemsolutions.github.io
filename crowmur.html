About CrowMur

CrowMur lets each user sign in with their own Google account, upload a lead list, write their message, and send it from their own Gmail, slowly and safely, with automatic follow-ups. Away Mode reads out-of-office replies and holds the follow-up until the person is back. Replies are sorted into Interested, Not interested and Stop, and anyone who replied, bounced or opted out is remembered and skipped in future uploads. A live spam score checks your message while you write, and every email includes an opt-out line and mailing address for CAN-SPAM compliance.

Who it’s for

  • Freelancers and agencies doing outreach
  • Small business owners finding new clients
  • Sales and business development teams
  • Virtual assistants running campaigns for clients

Key features

  • Sends from your own Gmail in safe, small batches
  • Away Mode: waits until out-of-office contacts are back
  • Shiny Pile: sorts replies into Interested, Not interested, Stop
  • Crow Memory: skips anyone who replied, bounced or opted out
  • Caw-Check: live spam score while you write
  • CAN-SPAM opt-out built in; leads stay in your own Drive
